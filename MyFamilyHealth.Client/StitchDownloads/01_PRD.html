Healthcare Platform Features The Healthcare Platform is designed to empower patients with control over their medical data, facilitate secure sharing, and provide intelligent assistance through AI. 2.1. Core Features Patient Data Management Centralized Record Keeping: Secure storage for all patient lab tests, X-rays, medical images, and other related health data. National ID and Phone Number Integration: Ability for medical centers to upload test results directly to patient profiles using national ID and phone number, eliminating the need for separate systems at the centers. Data Sharing and Access Control Family Sharing: Patients can securely share their health profiles with family members, granting controlled access to specific data. Doctor Access Request: Doctors can request access to a patient's profile to view relevant medical history and test results. Access will be granted and managed by the patient. Medication Management and Reminders Medication Tracking: Record of all taken medications, including those with potential issues. Smart Reminders: Alarms and notifications to remind patients when to take their medications. Parental Notifications: Automatic notifications to parents/guardians once medications are taken (for dependent patients). AI-Powered Insights and Assistance Test and Medication Analysis: An AI agent will analyze lab tests and medication data to provide comprehensive information and insights to the patient. Dosage Recommendations: AI-driven suggestions for medication amounts, considering patient-specific data and medical guidelines. Test Overview Interpretation: AI will provide an easy-to-understand overview of complex test results. Booking Rescheduling: AI agent can reschedule doctor appointments based on test results and patient availability. Doctor Communication: AI can send test results directly to doctors with a message, enabling them to assess the need for a revisit without an immediate in-person consultation. 2.2. Suggested Additional Features Telemedicine Integration: Seamless integration with video conferencing tools for virtual consultations. Wearable Device Integration: Connect with fitness trackers and smartwatches to collect real-time health data (e.g., heart rate, activity levels). Personalized Health Recommendations: AI-driven recommendations for lifestyle changes, diet, and exercise based on patient data. Emergency Medical Information (EMI): A quick-access section for critical medical information (allergies, blood type, emergency contacts) accessible by first responders. Prescription Management: Ability to request prescription refills directly through the app and integrate with pharmacies. Mental Health Support: Integration with mental health resources, including AI-powered chatbots for initial support or guided meditation. Insurance Claim Assistance: AI-powered assistance in understanding and processing insurance claims related to treatments and tests. Drug Interaction Checker: An AI feature to flag potential drug interactions based on the patient's current medications. Chronic Disease Management Programs: Tailored programs and monitoring for patients with chronic conditions, including AI-driven progress tracking and alerts.
